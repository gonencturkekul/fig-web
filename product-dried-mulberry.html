Dried mulberry

White mulberries left to ripen fully on the tree, then dried in the Anatolian sun and wind without any processing.

Request a quote See the whole range
  • Morus alba
  • Türkiye
  • HS 0813.40

Five kilos fresh, one kilo dried

Fresh mulberries are about 80% water. The summer sun and wind take nearly all of it away, which is why the sweetness is so concentrated.

Drying happens in the heat of the summer months: roughly five kilos of fresh fruit yield one kilo dried. Once drying is finished the fruit comes in to our plant, where it is fumigated, cleaned and packed, then prepared to whatever packaging and grammage you ask for.

  • Ripened to full maturity on the tree, dried without processing
  • Dried naturally by sun and wind, with no added sugar and no additives
  • Fumigated and cleaned at the plant before packing
  • Prepared to the packaging and grammage you request
