Dried Mulberry

Mulberries left to ripen fully on the tree, then dried in the Anatolian sun and wind without any processing.

Five kilos of fresh fruit, one kilo dried

Fresh mulberries are about 80% water by weight. Drying in the heat of the summer months, in the sun and the wind, takes nearly all of it away: roughly five kilos of fresh fruit yield one kilo dried. That is why the sweetness is so concentrated.

Once drying is finished the fruit comes in to our plant, where it is fumigated, cleaned and packed. It is then prepared to whatever packaging and grammage you ask for.

  • Ripened to full maturity on the tree, dried without processing
  • Dried naturally by sun and wind, with no added sugar and no additives
  • Fumigated and cleaned at the plant before packing
  • Prepared to the packaging and grammage you request
