Dried fig

Aydın, Aegean region

Whole natural figs, sun-dried and hand-graded into eight grades, from Lerida and Protoben to diced. Soft, honeyed and packed without additives.

Fig salami

Signature product

Pressed fig paste worked with spices or nuts into a firm log and cured until sliceable. Six varieties, all 180 g and free of added sugar.
Formats: 180 g logs · six varieties · mixed cartons

Your format, your label

We run our own packing line, so a pallet can leave as a supermarket-ready retail pack or as a plain industrial carton for further processing.

  • Retail: printed pouches, corrugated trays, PET jars and gift boxes.
  • Food service: 5 kg and 10 kg cartons with food-grade liners.
  • Industrial: 12.5 kg blocks, IQF-compatible and paste formats.
  • Private label: artwork, barcodes and multilingual labels handled in house.
Discuss your packaging

Built for pallet buyers

We are a wholesale supplier, not a shop. Everything here is set up around container loads, repeat programmes and the paperwork that has to travel with them.

Bulk formats

5 kg, 10 kg and 12.5 kg lined cardboard cartons as standard, wooden crates on request, stacked on heat-treated ISPM 15 pallets and stretch-wrapped for sea freight.

Private label

We print pouches, sleeves and cartons to your artwork, handle barcodes and multilingual nutrition panels, and hold the plates for repeat runs.

Export documentation

Certificate of origin, health certificate, analysis report and phytosanitary papers travel with every shipment. ATR and EUR.1 issued where applicable.

Order sizes

From one pallet of a stocked format up to full container loads. Mixed containers across the four product lines are welcome.

Terms and routes

EXW, FOB İzmir, CIF and DDP. Sea freight from İzmir, road groupage into Europe, air freight for samples and urgent top-ups.

Specification first

Send us your spec (calibre, moisture, SO₂ limit, packaging) and we will confirm in writing what we can hold, lot after lot.
