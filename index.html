We own the chain from tree to carton

Gardens we know by name

Most exporters buy at auction. We contract the same growing families every year, walk the gardens before harvest, and let the figs ripen and fall instead of picking early.

Every lot traced and tested

Each carton carries a code back to its grower and drying batch. Aflatoxin, pesticide residue and moisture are tested on every lot, and the certificates travel with the shipment.

Certified to BRCGS and ISO 22000, with EU and USDA organic lots.

Read our story

From the tree to your warehouse

  1. Picked

    Figs ripen and fall from the tree, and growers gather them every day through August and September.

  2. Sun-dried and graded

    Five to seven days on open trays, turned by hand, then metal detection, optical sorting, hand grading and lab tests.

  3. Packed and shipped

    Your format and label, palletised and loaded at İzmir, usually within 10 working days. FCL, LCL or air freight; EXW, FOB, CIF or DDP.

Dried fig specification

Two valleys, chosen on purpose

Büyük Menderes valley, Aydın

The Aegean interior around Nazilli and İncirliova is where the Sarılop fig became the reference for dried figs worldwide. Hot dry summers, cool nights and calcareous soil let the fruit ripen on the tree until the sugars concentrate on their own.

Figs for every grade · harvest August–September · dried and packed at our Nazilli facility

Malatya, Eastern Anatolia

Roughly four in five dried apricots eaten anywhere come from the terraces above the Euphrates around Malatya. Altitude and a short, intense season give the Hacıhaliloğlu apricot its density and keeping quality.

Golden and natural apricots · harvest July–August · bought directly from growers
